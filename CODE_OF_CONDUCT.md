# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the SmartStock project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, gender, disability, ethnicity, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or other personal characteristics.

We expect all contributors, maintainers, and participants to communicate professionally and respectfully.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

* Being respectful and considerate
* Giving constructive feedback
* Accepting different opinions and approaches
* Focusing criticism on ideas and implementations rather than individuals
* Helping other contributors learn and improve
* Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Harassment or discrimination
* Personal attacks or insults
* Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior
* Publishing private information without permission
* Threats or intimidation
* Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention
* Hate speech
* Deliberate disruption of the project or its contributors

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior.

Unacceptable behavior may result in actions including:

1. A warning
2. Removal of inappropriate content
3. Temporary restriction from project participation
4. Permanent restriction from project participation

The response will depend on the severity and circumstances of the incident.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainer through the contact method provided in the project's GitHub repository.

Please include:

* A description of the incident
* The date and location of the incident
* Relevant links, screenshots, or other evidence
* Any additional context that may help with the investigation

Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related spaces.
